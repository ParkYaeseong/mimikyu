About

I studied systems biotechnology and worked in biomedical labs and a biobank before moving into scientific software. That experience shapes how I build tools for researchers. I pay particular attention to how data was generated, which inputs and settings went into a run, and whether another person can review the result.

At KRIBB’s Biofoundry Project Office, my work spans biomolecular model pipelines, research notebooks, and GPU-backed services.
